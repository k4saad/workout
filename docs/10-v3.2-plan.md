# v3.2 — a run to look at, and a run to send

Two asks after the QA pass: the route animation on a run's page wants the
whole screen, and a run wants to leave the phone as a picture, the way
Strava does it.

## Phase E — the route, full screen

- The route card on a run's page (after a run, and in Progress → Runs)
  opens full screen from a tap anywhere on it or its **Full screen**
  button. It is pushed over the tab bar, like the runner and the tracker.
- Full screen, the map pans and pinches; the route draws itself in more
  slowly (2.4 s), and the replay runs half as long again. **Draw it
  again**, pace colours, replay and share sit along the bottom; the title,
  date, distance and time across the top with the close button.
- The camera frames the route clear of both. Reduced motion jumps.

## Phase F — the share card

- A 4:5 card, rendered as a **1080 × 1350 PNG**: the app's mark, the run's
  name and date, the route as a glowing crimson line on black, distance,
  time and pace. With the route off, the distance fills the card, with
  climb, time and pace under it.
- **Privacy.** A GPS trail is a home address, so the card is built to leave
  it behind: the route is a line with **no map and no coordinates**, and
  the **first and last 200 m are cut** (`privateRoute`) unless he turns that
  off. A run too short to hide both ends shows the numbers instead. Nothing
  leaves unless he presses Share; the PNG goes to the app's cache and the
  phone's share sheet (no new dependency, no new permission).
- CLAUDE.md's trail rule now names this as the one exception to "only in
  a backup", and `test/privacy_audit_test.dart` holds the card to it: it
  may not import the map, the location plugin or the link launcher, may
  not read the stored start/end coordinates, and must go through
  `privateRoute`.
